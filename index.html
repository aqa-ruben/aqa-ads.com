AQA Ads is a small paid-media practice run by Ruben Colon. We plan, manage, and report Meta (Facebook and Instagram) campaigns for ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands.

What we do

01

Meta ad strategy

Account structure, audiences, and budget across the products and offers the brand is selling, on Facebook and Instagram.

02

Campaign setup and management

Campaigns are built in the client’s Meta ad account, then reviewed and adjusted on the cadence that engagement includes.

03

Creative direction and testing

Direction for images, video, and copy, and a testing plan so new ads can replace ones that have worn out.

04

Market and customer research

Research on the customer, the category, and the offer, used to brief creative and to read the account in context.

05

Reporting

A record of what ran, what it cost, and what changed, on the cadence for that engagement.
