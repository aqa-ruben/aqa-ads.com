Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy explains cancellation and refunds for AQA Ads services. A signed services agreement takes precedence where it differs from this policy. The Terms of Service cover the rest of the engagement.

Monthly retainers

Monthly retainers, including Light Touch and Full Service, can be cancelled by written notice sent to ruben@aqa-ads.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. The retainer stays in place, and the fee for that period stays due, until that date.

Work already performed, and project fees

Fees for work already performed are non-refundable. One-time project fees are non-refundable once work has begun.

Ad spend

Ad spend is billed by the advertising platform, such as Meta, to the payment method on the client’s ad account. Ad spend is not refundable by AQA Ads. Questions about a charge from the platform go to that platform.

Billing questions

For billing questions, email ruben@aqa-ads.com.
