Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

AQA Ads (“AQA Ads,” “we,” “us”) is a paid-media practice run by Ruben Colon. This policy describes how we handle personal information in connection with this website and our services to business clients.

Information we collect

Contact information you send by email. If you write to ruben@aqa-ads.com, we receive whatever you include: your email address, your name if you give it, and the contents of the message.

Client business and ad-account data. When a client engages AQA Ads, we use the business information and advertising-account data needed to do the work. That can include access to the ad account, campaign and performance data, and product or customer research the client shares. We use this information only to deliver the services, to administer the engagement, and to meet legal duties.

Payment information. Service fees are processed by Stripe. AQA Ads does not store card numbers or other full payment-card data. Stripe collects payment details under its own terms and privacy policy.

This website has no accounts, contact forms, or analytics cookies. We do not ask visitors to create a profile here.

How we use information

We use information to answer inquiries, provide the services, invoice and keep records, protect our accounts, and comply with the law. We do not use a client’s ad-account data to advertise AQA Ads to that client’s customers.

Selling personal information

AQA Ads does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Third-party processors

We use a short list of service providers to run the practice:

  • Stripe, to invoice clients and process payments.
  • Google Workspace, for email at ruben@aqa-ads.com and related business communication.
  • Meta, the advertising platform where client campaigns run. Campaign data is processed in the client’s ad account under Meta’s terms.

Stripe and Google Workspace process information to provide those services to AQA Ads. Meta processes campaign data as the platform the client uses for advertising. Each provider’s own privacy policy describes its handling of that information.

Data retention

We keep inquiry emails and client records for as long as we need them to provide the services, keep accurate business and tax records, and resolve disputes. When an engagement is over and we no longer need client materials for those reasons, we delete them or return them, unless the law requires us to keep them. Stripe retains payment records under its own policies. We keep the invoice and accounting records we need for the business.

Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses. Email ruben@aqa-ads.com with your request. We will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm that the request is yours. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

This policy does not cover websites or platforms we do not control, including Meta and Stripe.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
